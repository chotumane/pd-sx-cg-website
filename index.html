Areas of expertise

A short guide for parents on the conditions Dr Shobhita treats. Every child is different, so please book a consultation for advice about your child.

  • General neonatal surgery

    Surgical care for newborns in their first month, including premature and unwell babies. Seek care urgently if a newborn vomits green, has a swollen belly, or is not passing stool.

  • General paediatric surgery

    Common surgical problems in children such as hernias, undescended testis, appendicitis, lumps and swellings. Many are planned day-care or short-stay operations.

  • Congenital malformations

    Problems a baby is born with, for example in the food pipe, intestines, anus, abdominal wall or diaphragm. Some are first noticed on a pregnancy scan.

  • Neonatal deformities

    Visible differences noticed at birth, such as unusual swellings or shape. An early check tells you whether treatment is needed and when.

  • Birth defect correction

    Planned corrective surgery, with the timing, the steps and the recovery explained to parents beforehand so there are no surprises.

  • Paediatric trauma

    Injuries from falls, accidents and swallowed objects. In an emergency go to the nearest hospital casualty first; do not wait for a clinic appointment.

  • Paediatric burn management

    Scalds from hot liquids are the commonest burns in young children. Cool the burn under running water for about 20 minutes, do not apply toothpaste, butter or ointments, and go to a hospital.

  • Prenatal counselling and management

    If a pregnancy scan shows a problem in the baby, meet before delivery to understand the condition and plan where and how the baby is born and treated.

  • Second opinion

    Unsure about a diagnosis or advised surgery? Bring all reports, scans and earlier prescriptions for a clear, unhurried review.

Please bring your child's earlier reports, scans, prescriptions and birth or discharge papers to the consultation.
